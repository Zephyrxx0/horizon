| T-1-18 | Tampering | Hostile image payloads (decompression bombs) | mitigate | 5MB raw pre-check BEFORE createImageBitmap; canvas dimensions floored at 2048px; decode failure → honest error not crash |
| T-1-19 | Denial of Service | Storage quota exhaustion from repeated uploads | mitigate | ≤2MB compression pre-write; usage/quota monitor at 0.8 threshold showing UI-SPEC warning; requestPersistentStorage opportunistically; failures surfaced via persistent Toast (Pitfall 4 protocol) |
